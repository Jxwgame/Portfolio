01 · HOME

The shortcuts a working day needs

The screen the front desk keeps open for the whole shift, gathering the actions the day calls for into a single place.

1

Occupancy, rooms sold, guests in house, and the rooms housekeeping still has to clean.

2

Walk-in sale, day-use stay, advance booking, check-in. One click each, no menu hunting.

3

Rooms approaching check-out time, the ones already overdue, and a check-out button on every row.

03 · BOOKINGS

Two views of the same nights

The schedule lays every stay out as rooms against dates so what is free reads at a glance. The booking form checks a date range, then creates the stay against the rate plan active for those nights, with a manual override when the front desk needs one.
